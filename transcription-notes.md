# Transcription notes

## 1. Source
Page images: 468 JPEGs (about 2000 px wide, 2.5 GB) in `source-images/` (gitignored), downloaded 2026-11-05 with `tools/download_images.py` from the Archive Viewer listing of the Joseph Smith Papers images (`9153_chl_bom_pm_NNN_download.jpg`; `000`, `000-01`... are the unnumbered leaves before the paginated pages). `source-images/manifest.tsv` lists them in order. There is no PDF: `prepare_bands.py` reads `source-images/` and the 3-digit page number used everywhere is the image's position in the manifest (1 = `_000`, 2 = `_000-01`, 3 = `_000-02`, 4 = `_000-03`, 5 = `_001`, ...). Original: Printer's Manuscript of the Book of Mormon, ca. Aug 1829-Jan 1830, Church History Library; scribes Oliver Cowdery, "scribe 2", Hyrum Smith; 466 pages. Reference for scoring only: Joseph Smith Papers online transcript (original inscription only; leaves out the 1830 and 1837 marks).

## 2. Conventions
See `tools/transcription-spec.md`. Layers: base text, `@1837` / `@1830` / `@later` suffixes on later-layer marks.
